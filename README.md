# Retail Sales & Customer Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales performance across products, customers, countries, and time.

The Power BI dashboard provides insights into overall sales and profitability, product performance, geographic distribution, customer demographics, and purchasing patterns to better understand the major drivers of business performance.

## Business Question

How is the business performing across products, customers, countries, and time, and which areas contribute most to sales and profitability?

## Key Insights

- The business generated approximately **$43.20 million in total sales** and **$20.54 million in profit**, representing a profit margin of approximately **47.54%**.

- Approximately **198,000 units** were sold across the product categories analyzed.

- **Computers** were the strongest-performing product category by sales, generating considerably more revenue than the other categories.

- The **United States** was the largest geographic market, contributing approximately **$23.27 million**, or **53.87% of total sales**.

- Sales increased considerably between **2016 and 2019**, reaching their highest level in 2019 before declining in subsequent years. The sharp decline in 2021 should be interpreted cautiously if the dataset contains only partial-year records.

- The customer base was almost evenly distributed by gender, with approximately **50.75% male and 49.25% female customers**.

- The United States also had the largest customer base among the countries analyzed, which corresponds with its position as the leading market by total sales.

## Recommendations

- The business should continue monitoring the **Computers category**, as it represents a major contributor to overall sales performance.

- Markets outside the United States should be investigated for potential growth opportunities to reduce dependence on a single dominant geographic market.

- Lower-performing product categories should be examined to determine whether their performance is driven by lower demand, pricing, product availability, or other factors.

- Customer purchasing patterns should be analyzed beyond demographic characteristics to identify valuable customer segments and opportunities for targeted marketing.

- The decline in sales after 2019 should be investigated further before drawing conclusions about business performance, particularly to confirm whether later years contain complete sales records.
